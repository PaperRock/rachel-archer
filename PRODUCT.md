## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (chosen by the user; static-first, mirrors the sibling sites in this folder). This repo is a from-scratch rebuild of the incumbent Wix site, not a port of it.

## Users

Adults in and around Maidstone, Kent — and online clients anywhere — who are struggling with anxiety, depression, stress, grief, anger, relationship problems, or major life transitions, and are deciding whether to start counselling. Their situation: taking the first step feels daunting, so they are looking for someone who feels safe and warm before they reach out. Their job is to assess whether Rachel is the right fit and get up the courage to make contact. A secondary audience: people who cannot attend in person (too far away, housebound, ill, or childcare-constrained) and need online counselling instead.

## Product Purpose

The website is the shopfront of Rachel Archer Counselling, a private BACP-registered counselling practice in Maidstone. It exists to reassure prospective clients that Rachel is warm, professional, and trustworthy, and to move them to make first contact by phone, email, or form. Success is a qualified enquiry, answered within 48 hours.

## Positioning

A warm, human, individual practice in deliberate contrast to clinical or corporate therapy services. Rachel is a BACP-registered integrative and humanistic counsellor, IFS-informed (parts work), who builds therapy around each person rather than a one-size-fits-all model. The sharpest claims: genuine connection ("Let's talk about it"), a truly individual service, and a reduced fee for students, low-income clients, and NHS staff.

## Operating Context

Counselling happens in person at Cave Hill, Maidstone (ME15 6DX) or online via phone, Skype, FaceTime, or WhatsApp. Sessions are 1 hour. Clients make first contact by phone (07915 101 997) or email (rachelarchertherapy@gmail.com) and are promised a reply within 48 hours. The practice is active on Instagram (@rachelarchertherapy) and Facebook. Rachel is a registered member of the BACP and abides by their Ethical Framework. Meta description of the incumbent site: "Feeling stuck, anxious or experiencing relationship problems - I can help."

## Product Principles

1. Put the visitor at ease first — the audience is anxious about reaching out; warmth and safety outrank persuasion.
2. Move people to contact — the site's job is a first enquiry, so phone, email, and form must always be one step away.
3. Keep claims honest — everything must be true of the practice and its published approach; nothing about outcomes may overpromise.
4. Preserve the trust markers — BACP registration, the Ethical Framework, the professional history, and the confirmed contact/fee facts are non-negotiable.
5. Serve both journeys equally — in-person (Maidstone) and online clients are both first-class and must each see their path clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific accessibility standard was established. The confirmed audience — people with anxiety, panic attacks, and clients who cannot leave the house — sets a high bar for a calm, low-friction, mobile-first experience; a privacy policy exists on the incumbent site and should be carried over.
