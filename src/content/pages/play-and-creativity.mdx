<Section alt>

    <div slot="left">
      <h2>Why words aren’t always enough</h2>
    </div>
    <Prose slot="right">
      <p>Talk therapy is a wonderful tool, but our deepest emotional experiences, early memories, and subconscious protective patterns don’t always live in the analytical, language-driven parts of our brain.</p>

      <ul>
        <li><strong>Accessing the subconscious:</strong> Creative media act as a bridge between the conscious mind and non-verbal feelings, allowing deeply held patterns to surface naturally and safely.</li>
        <li><strong>Creating safe distance:</strong> Expressing an emotion or experience through a drawing, a symbol, or a visual metaphor provides just enough distance to explore painful topics without feeling overwhelmed.</li>
        <li><strong>Reconnecting with wholeness:</strong> Creative tools engage the whole brain, combining sensory experience with insight to create lasting internal shifts.</li>
      </ul>
    </Prose>
  </Split>
</Section>

<Section>
  <Split>
    <div slot="left">
      <h2>How creativity and play show up in session</h2>
    </div>
    <Prose slot="right">
      <p>Creative therapy isn’t about artistic skill or “doing it right” — there is no pressure to perform. It is entirely about the process and what it reveals to you. If you are open to exploring creatively, sessions can adapt flexibly to what feels right in the moment:</p>

      <ul>
        <li><strong>Visualizing parts:</strong> Using imagery, colors, or figurines to give a voice to different internal experiences or protective parts.</li>
        <li><strong>Sand tray & symbols:</strong> Creating physical representations of your inner landscape using miniature objects to gain fresh perspective on complex relationships or life transitions.</li>
        <li><strong>Metaphor & story:</strong> Tapping into symbolic language to reframe long-standing narratives and explore new possibilities for growth.</li>
        <li><strong>Journaling & expressive drawing:</strong> Externalizing internal noise onto paper to foster clarity, grounding, and relief.</li>
      </ul>
    </Prose>
  </Split>
</Section>

<Section alt>
  <Split>
    <div slot="left">
      <h2>The benefits of a playful, creative approach</h2>
    </div>
    <Prose slot="right">
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Deeper insight, faster:</strong> Symbolism and imagery often cut straight to the core of an issue faster than traditional dialogue.</li>
        <li><strong>Reduced stress & defense:</strong> Playful curiosity lowers hyper-vigilance, making hard conversations feel lighter, more approachable, and less intimidating.</li>
        <li><strong>Enhanced emotional resilience:</strong> Engaging your creative mind fosters flexibility, problem-solving, and a renewed sense of personal agency.</li>
        <li><strong>A safe space for all ages:</strong> A creative, adaptable environment honours both the mature adult navigating complex life realities and the younger inner parts that simply need to be heard and held.</li>
      </ul>

      <p>Therapy works best when tailored dynamically to your unique inner world. By welcoming creative tools into the space, we open up dynamic, transformative ways to heal, grow, and reconnect with who you truly are.</p>
    </Prose>
  </Split>
